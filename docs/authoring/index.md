This is the workflow for building guardrails — the how-to quadrant, distinct from
the *ideas* ([Concepts](/concepts)) and the *specs* ([Reference](/reference)).

- **[Write a guardrail](/authoring/write-a-guardrail)** — the folder, the
  declaration, the check.
- **[Prove it fires](/authoring/prove-it-fires)** — the step that turns a hope into
  enforcement. A rule that loads but never refuses is worse than no rule.
- **[Disable a guardrail](/authoring/disable-a-guardrail)** — switch one off,
  including a rule a plugin shipped.
- **[Troubleshooting](/authoring/troubleshooting)** — a rule didn't fire, an
  unexpected refusal, or two rules colliding.

New to it? The [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) walks the whole loop once,
end to end.
